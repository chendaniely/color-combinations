What the numbers actually show. The two methods disagree badly — they overlap on only 9 and 8 colours respectively. So this isn't a cosmetic choice; you'd be shipping meaningfully different advice. Way 1 returns more for Persona A and about the same for Persona B, but the counts aren't the point: both are tunable to any size. The difference that can't be tuned away is where the numbers come from. Way 1's come from your face. Way 2's come from me.
A separate finding worth knowing. Sanzo Wada's palette leans warm — 109 of the 157 colours read warm, only 48 cool. Whatever we build, cool-toned visitors will get a smaller personal palette than warm-toned ones. That's the book, not the method. We should say so on the page rather than let it look like a bug.
