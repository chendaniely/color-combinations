Pick a color — disc mockups

Round run during the Session 11 brainstorm to settle one question: should the picker's disc plot the book's 157 colors on top of the hue/saturation field? All four discs below are live — drag them, and the brightness slider drives each panel independently.

Outcome: the picker ships as A, a plain disc. Seeing the overlay live is what settled it — on a control whose whole job is aiming at a color, the dots compete with the target instead of helping you hit it. B and D were not thrown away: the owner liked the plot itself, so it moves to the Browse page as a separate "map of the book" feature, where reading the collection is the job. Kept here as that feature's starting point.
